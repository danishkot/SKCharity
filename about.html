Our History

Established in 2023, DaNaNas Corp. is a non-governmental organization (NGO) dedicated to using information technology (IT) for social good. Our mission is to bridge the digital divide and support underprivileged communities by providing technology-driven charity initiatives, IT education, and digital empowerment programs.

At DaNaNas Corp., we believe that access to technology is a fundamental right. We work tirelessly to ensure that schools, orphanages, and low-income communities have the necessary resources to learn, grow, and thrive in the digital world. Through donation campaigns, free IT training, and community outreach projects, we strive to create a brighter future for all.

Join us in making a difference! Together, we can use technology to empower, educate, and uplift those in need.
