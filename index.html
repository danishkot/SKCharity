Our Mission

At SumbangKasih, our mission is to:

  • Empower Underserved Communities – Provide access to technology, digital education, and IT resources to underprivileged individuals and organizations.
  • Bridge the Digital Divide – Ensure that everyone, regardless of background, has the opportunity to learn and grow through technology-driven initiatives.
  • Support Charitable Causes – Organize fundraising, donation drives, and volunteer programs to help communities in need through the power of IT.
  • Promote Digital Literacy – Conduct free IT training, coding workshops, and cybersecurity awareness programs to equip people with essential digital skills.
  • Foster Innovation for Good – Develop and support technology solutions that address social issues, improve quality of life, and create long-term impact.
